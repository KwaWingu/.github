# KwaWingu

**Run your property, restaurant or safari business from one cloud.**
Mobile-first software for East African operators, with mobile money and multi-currency payments built in, and WhatsApp AI agents that qualify, quote and take payment inside the chat your customers already use. Built in Arusha, Tanzania.

🌍 [kwawingu.com](https://kwawingu.com) · ✉️ info@kwawingu.com

---

### Products

| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| **[KwaWingu Tours](https://tours.kwawingu.com)** | Booking and operations for tour operators: reservations, OTA channel management (Viator, GetYourGuide) and multi-currency payments |
| **[KwaWingu Stay](https://stay.kwawingu.com)** | Rates and distribution for lodges, camps and hotels: publish contract rate sheets once, and every linked tour operator quotes from the live version |
| **[KwaWingu Dine](https://dine.kwawingu.com)** | Restaurant and F&B management: digital menus, dine-in, takeaway and WhatsApp ordering, and a kitchen display |
| **[KwaWingu Rentals](https://rentals.kwawingu.com)** | Property management for landlords: digital tenant onboarding, mobile-money rent collection and a live occupancy dashboard |
| **[KwaWingu QR](https://qr.kwawingu.com)** | Dynamic QR codes for business: edit destinations after printing, with scan analytics and branded codes |
| **[KwaWingu Scan](https://scan.kwawingu.com)** | Free online presence checker: scores tour operators, lodges, hotels and restaurants out of 100, with a fix list and public rankings |

All six products share one login, one billing layer and the same payment rails: M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Mixx by Yas, HaloPesa and cards.

### Open source

- **[Mobile-Payments-Library](https://github.com/KwaWingu/Mobile-Payments-Library)**: Java 21 SDK for Tanzanian mobile money (M-Pesa, Airtel Money, HaloPesa, Mixx by Yas)
- **[kw-wp-plugin](https://github.com/KwaWingu/kw-wp-plugin)**: WordPress plugin that turns a KwaWingu Tours catalogue into an SEO-ready tour-operator website
